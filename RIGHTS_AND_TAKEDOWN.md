# 权利异议处理与侵权配合说明 / Rights concerns and takedown cooperation

## 简体中文

本项目尊重 MT 管理器及其他开发者, 设计者和权利人的合法权益. [设计参考与致谢](docs/design-reference.md) 用于说明来源, 不构成已获授权或不存在侵权的承诺, 也不限制权利人通过其他适用渠道提出请求.

### 如何联系

请通过本仓库的 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/SuperMonster003/AutoJs6-Plugin-Three-Color-Picker/issues) 提交权利异议, 标题建议使用 `[Rights concern]` 或 `[权利异议]`. 请尽量提供以下信息, 便于定位和沟通:

- 涉及的原作品, 名称, 官方来源或其他可核实的权利依据.
- 本项目中需要核查的具体文件, 资源, 页面或版本; 必要时提供最小范围的截图对照.
- 您与相关权利的关系及希望采取的措施, 如更正署名, 补充说明, 替换, 移除或停止分发.
- 可用于后续沟通的渠道; 使用 GitHub 回复即可, 无需公开私人联系方式.

Issues 为公开渠道. 请不要上传身份证件, 私人联系方式, 未公开作品, 密钥或与请求无关的个人信息. 如必须交换非公开材料, 请先仅说明需要私下沟通, 双方确认适当渠道后再提供必要材料. 本项目不要求将敏感证明公开作为受理前提.

### 我们如何处理

1. 阅读请求并定位涉及的版本与内容. 信息不足时, 说明需要补充的最小信息, 不预先把异议视为无效.
2. 与提出请求的权利人沟通, 核对来源, 权利归属, 使用方式及可能适用的授权.
3. 根据核实情况配合更正署名或说明, 更换设计或资源, 移除内容, 或暂停相关内容的分发. 需要先行减少争议内容传播时, 在可控制范围内采取相应措施.
4. 告知采取的措施与涉及版本, 在适当位置保留不含私人信息的变更记录. 若仍有争议, 继续沟通并说明尚未解决的事项.

本说明不预先认定任何侵权事实, 不替代双方的具体沟通或授权约定. 已发布副本可能存在于第三方镜像或用户设备上, 我们会说明可控制的处理范围, 不承诺删除无法控制的所有副本. 项目许可证与第三方权利的适用边界保持不变.

## English

We respect the rights of MT Manager and other developers, designers and rights holders. The [design acknowledgement](docs/design-reference.md) records a reference; it is not a representation of permission or a guarantee of non-infringement, and it does not restrict other available channels for a request.

### Contact

Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/SuperMonster003/AutoJs6-Plugin-Three-Color-Picker/issues), preferably titled `[Rights concern]`, identifying the original work and a verifiable source, the relevant project files, assets, screens or versions, your relationship to the rights, and the requested action. GitHub replies are sufficient for follow-up; personal contact details need not be public.

Issues are public. Do not upload identity documents, private contact details, unpublished works, secrets or unrelated personal information. If private evidence is necessary, first request an appropriate private communication channel and agree on it before sharing the minimum needed material. Public disclosure of sensitive evidence is not a prerequisite for review.

### Response

1. Review and locate the reported content, asking only for missing information needed to investigate.
2. Discuss the source, rights, use and any applicable permission with the reporting rights holder.
3. Cooperate on appropriate attribution corrections, clarification, replacement, removal or suspension of distribution. Where interim measures are needed, address disputed distribution within our control.
4. Communicate the action and affected versions, keep an appropriate record without private information, and identify any unresolved points for further discussion.

This process does not prejudge infringement or replace specific communication or permission. Copies may exist on third-party mirrors or users' devices; we will explain the scope within our control rather than promise removal of every copy. The scope of the project license and third-party rights remains unchanged.
